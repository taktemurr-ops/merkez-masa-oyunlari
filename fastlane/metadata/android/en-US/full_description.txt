Board Games brings 26 board games and 30 variants together in one app, with our own board and piece design. Strategy classics like chess and draughts, backgammon and dice games, mancala and quick games; every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Strategy classics: Chess, Checkers, Turkish Draughts, Go, Reversi, Nine Men's Morris, Mangala, Hnefatafl, Alquerque, Surakarta, Halma, Chinese Checkers
• Lines and connections: Noughts & Crosses, Four in a Row, Five in a Row, Five & Capture, Six in a Row, Hex, Rings & Bridges, Dots and Boxes
• Dice and racing: Backgammon, Don't Get Angry, Snakes & Ladders, Five Dice, Farkle, Liar's Dice
• Variant packs: Chess Variety (9), Draughts Variety (8), Sowing Games (8), Backgammon Variety (5)

Highlights
• 50,000 levels per game; openings and rival strength grow step by step, and every level is checked by a strong AI.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, master rivals later.
• Hints and undo; in dice games the dice roll automatically.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; dice are just part of the game.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
